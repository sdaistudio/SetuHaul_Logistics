SetuHaul Logistics — driver exception service

When many drivers report delays at once, SetuHaul answers each one in English, Hindi or Hinglish with a dock slot the truck can really make, and never promises the same dock time twice. Rules read the message; an exact, proven-optimal search plans the docks; the database refuses any conflicting booking; a person decides when no safe answer exists.

Built on the 4 August 2026 Jaipur snapshot for the FDE Academy challenge.

Updated after the 18 November 2026 review. The hand-ins below (v2) reconcile our earlier design with a second one: pick = book in one transaction, earmarks for priority loads, a lean pilot at about US$22–30 a month, and an exact scheduling engine with a plain reason for every truck.
