## What the agents did

- 162 bookings made automatically (safe class), 86 sent to the coordinator, 19 escalated.
- A full re-plan that moves working bookings was proposed to the coordinator in 27 of 40 days.
- 0 trucks were still booked on a broken dock after the run because their re-booking depends on a move only a person may approve; every one was in the coordinator's queue.
- Planner time p50 1114 ms, p95 3065 ms; whole agent run p50 3.85 s, p95 4.94 s.
- Exact solver cross-checked against OR-Tools CP-SAT on 40 proven-optimal repair problems.
