Dock-ready time = GPS-predicted arrival + gate-to-dock time. If GPS is silent, the driver's declared ETA plus a confidence buffer is used instead, and the FASTag toll watchdog checks whether the truck crossed the next plaza when expected. Thresholds (tolerance, yard wait, slip, GPS-silent minutes) are variables in rules/rules.yaml → control_tower. The GPS is polled on demand only (see the location ladder in the solution design), so the colour updates on each fix, ETA message or booking change.

Possible slots. For a truck that is late (dock-ready more than 15 min into its window), early (dock-ready 30 min or more before its window), waiting in the yard past its window, or without a booking, the column next to Act lists slots it can really take now: the same feasibility rules as the driver chat (dock type, reefer, weight, breakdowns, slots already taken, 21:00 rule), using the GPS-predicted arrival when GPS is live. The first is the look-ahead recommendation (best for every truck at the facility); slots that would leave another truck with no dock are never suggested. The coordinator can pick another, or Modify to any feasible slot and any start inside its window; the WhatsApp message follows the choice. Nothing is booked until the driver replies YES, and the booking is re-checked then.

WhatsApp buttons open wa.me with a pre-written message the coordinator can edit before sending. In production the same templates go through the WhatsApp Business API as approved message templates, and every send is logged on the shipment.
